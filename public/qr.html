CREATE A QR CODE
Paste a URL to get a QR code for it — no short link is created. The QR code points straight at the URL you enter.

YOUR QR CODES
The QR codes you've made, one card per URL, saved to your account so they're here on any device you sign in on. Click the QR icon on a card to show its code, or delete the ones you no longer need.

YOUR TINY URLS
Every short URL you've created, one card each, so you can get a QR code for any of them. They're managed on the Redirects page, so they can't be deleted here.
