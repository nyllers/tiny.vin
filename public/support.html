Support

tiny.vin is a small, personal URL-shortening tool, including its Android and iOS apps. If something's broken or you have a question, get in touch.

Contact

Email anders.nyllet.nylund@gmail.com with what happened, including your device and app version if it's about the Android or iOS app.

Using the apps

Both apps work the same way: sign in with the Google account you use on tiny.vin (or paste an API key generated at tiny.vin/api), then share any link to the app from your device's share menu to shorten it. The shortened link is copied to your clipboard automatically.

Account issues

For anything about your tiny.vin account or data — including deleting it — see the Privacy Policy, or just email the address above.
