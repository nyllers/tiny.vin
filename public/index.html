CREATE A REDIRECT
Paste a URL to get a short link, or an e-mail address to create an alias that forwards to it — the type is detected automatically as you type, so there's nothing to pick. Custom paths, subdomains, and chosen e-mail aliases are added afterward, from the cards below.

YOUR REDIRECTS
Every redirect you've created, grouped one card per destination. The faint icon in the middle of each card shows whether it's a URL or an e-mail alias — use the filter below to show just one type. Each card's buttons attach another path, subdomain, or alias to that same destination.
